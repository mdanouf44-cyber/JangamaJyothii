# Screenshot Collection Guide 📸
## Take Your Own Screenshots While Setting Up Gmail

This guide will help you document your Gmail setup process with screenshots for future reference.

---

## 📱 **How to Take Screenshots:**

### **Windows:**
- **Full Screen**: Press `Windows + Print Screen`
- **Partial Screen**: Press `Windows + Shift + S`
- **Active Window**: Press `Alt + Print Screen`

### **Mac:**
- **Full Screen**: Press `Cmd + Shift + 3`
- **Partial Screen**: Press `Cmd + Shift + 4`
- **Window**: Press `Cmd + Shift + 4`, then `Space`

### **Mobile:**
- **iPhone**: Press `Volume Up + Side Button`
- **Android**: Press `Volume Down + Power Button`

## 🔧 **Pro Tips for Screenshots:**

### **Quality Tips:**
- **Full resolution** - Don't crop too much
- **Clear text** - Make sure all text is readable
- **Highlight important areas** - Use arrows or circles
- **Good lighting** - For mobile screenshots

### **Privacy Tips:**
- **Hide personal emails** - Blur out other email addresses
- **Cover passwords** - Never show actual passwords
- **Remove sensitive info** - Hide phone numbers, addresses
